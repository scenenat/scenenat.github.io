S C E N E NAT : Masked Generative Modeling for Language-Guided Indoor Scene Synthesis

Jeongjun Choi†   Yeonsoo Park†   H. Jin Kim  
Seoul National University   
†Co-First Authors   
Paper arXiv Code (Coming Soon)

SceneNAT generates realistic, physically plausible 3D indoor scenes from natural language instructions in a single pass, utilizing Masked Generative Modeling for fast and precise spatial control.

Abstract

The synthesis of realistic 3D indoor layouts from natural language is a long-standing challenge. While recent diffusion models and LLM-based agentic frameworks excel at generating diverse scenes, they suffer from prohibitive latency due to iterative sampling or multi-step optimization loops.

To address this, we introduce SceneNAT, a Non-Autoregressive Transformer (NAT) specifically designed for language-guided 3D scene synthesis. By treating scene generation as a parallel decoding process, SceneNAT enables fast and scalable inference. Furthermore, by leveraging the pre-trained knowledge of the CLIP embedding space, our model inherently generalizes to unseen vocabularies and abstract functional descriptions without relying on heavy external modules.

Generalization Ability via CLIP Semantic Space

Demonstrating robustness to free-form, ambiguous, and "in-the-wild" human instructions is an essential part of validating the practical applicability of a language-conditioned generation model. While our training strategy utilizes a templated dataset to learn precise spatial control, SceneNAT inherently generalizes to unseen expressions by leveraging the semantic alignment within the CLIP embedding space.

SceneNAT effectively interprets open-vocabulary or abstract functional descriptions by mapping them to visually associable concepts in the embedding space (e.g., "workspace" → desk/chair, "wine gathering" → wine cabinet). Consequently, the Scene Decoder synthesizes the scene based on these representations, applying learned spatial priors to arrange the objects plausibly.

This demonstrates that the model can accommodate semantic variations solely through the pre-trained knowledge of the text encoder, without requiring additional training for new vocabularies. Furthermore, for highly complex spatial logic (e.g., "create a separation"), our framework allows for seamless extensibility where a standard LLM can act as a simple pre-processor, maintaining SceneNAT as a lightweight and efficient 3D generation backend.

Fast and Scalable Inference

Unlike recent agentic frameworks (e.g., SceneWeaver, SAGE) that rely on slow, multi-step generation-critic loops, or diffusion-based models that require iterative denoising, SceneNAT decodes all scene elements in parallel.

This Non-Autoregressive approach drastically reduces latency, making SceneNAT highly suitable for real-time applications and scalable interactive environments, without sacrificing the physical plausibility or diversity of the generated 3D layouts.
